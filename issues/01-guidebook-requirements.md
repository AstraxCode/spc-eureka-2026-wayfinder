## Question

What exactly does the SPC Eureka! ITB 2026 guidebook demand of a full paper? Extract from
`gb-spc-rev1 (1).pdf` (22 pages, scanned) and report, quoting the relevant passage with its page
number for each item:

1. How the "must solve a problem" criterion is stated and weighted — does a comparison-only paper
   lose points, and what counts as a solution?
2. Page/word limits, mandatory sections, required language, formatting.
3. Rules on changing title, abstract, method or topic AFTER extended-abstract acceptance — what is
   locked, what is open, and by when.
4. Citation/reference requirements.
5. Submission mechanics and every deadline (abstract vs full paper vs final).

## Answer

Full findings: `../research/01-guidebook-requirements.md` (verbatim quotes + PDF page numbers for every item). Key results:

1. **"Must solve a problem"**: no single rubric row; stated as physics-linkage hard rule (p. 5 item 5) + theme framing (p. 3). **No comparison-only penalty is stated anywhere — do not infer one.** Full-Paper rubric weights: Analisis dan Pembahasan 25%, Metodologi 20%, Inovasi dan Orisinalitas 20%, Feasibility 15%, Dasar Teori 10%, Sistematika Penulisan 10%. Design/software/simulation are explicitly permitted output forms (p. 5 item 6); prototype NOT required at semifinal stage (FAQ Q8, p. 21).
2. **Format**: PDF, Bahasa Indonesia, Times New Roman 12pt, spacing 1.15, A4, 2.54 cm margins, 10–20 pages excl. cover/bibliography/appendices, filename `SPC_FullPaper_NamaKetuaTim.pdf` (p. 15). No word limit stated. 9 mandatory sections (p. 15). Format checked before grading (p. 12 item 5).
3. **Post-EA changes** (p. 12 item 3): idea/methodology/design changes allowed "selama tidak mengubah substansi utama proyek yang diajukan". Title/abstract rules and change deadline: **not addressed in the guidebook** — open question for the admin. One EA per team (p. 11 item 4).
4. **Citations**: Daftar Pustaka mandatory; citation practice scored within Sistematika (10%) and Dasar Teori (10%); academic-integrity rule bans fabrication/falsification (p. 5 item 7). Citation style not specified — template is the authority.
5. **Mechanics/deadlines**: submit via eurekaitb.com. Full Paper window **11–23 Oct 2026**; re-registration+payment 8 Oct; Final 28–29 Nov (Full Paper 40% + Presentation 60%); only 10 teams advance (p. 12 item 7). No separate final-paper deadline.

**2026-10-14 (admin ruling, supersedes the guidebook gap):** edits to the **title are allowed**;
the **Pendahuluan and the core problem being solved are prohibited from changing**. This resolves
the "not addressed in the guidebook" item — the admin's word is the authority. The repositioning
(sections after Pendahuluan) proceeds within that boundary.
